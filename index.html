What CATS Insights is

CATS Insights exists to give students the platform, the mentoring and the editorial infrastructure to produce academic work of genuine rigour — and then to publish it properly, with attribution, a citable record and permanent preservation.

Every article is written by a student. Every article is read, questioned and fact-checked by a student editorial board before it is published. The standard applied is the one that would be applied to any serious publication: claims are sourced, citations are verified against the original papers, and nothing appears that cannot be defended.
