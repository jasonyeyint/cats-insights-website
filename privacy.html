Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Who the data controller is

The Worthgate School, 68 New Dover Road, Canterbury, Kent, CT1 3LQ, United Kingdom, is the data controller for personal data published by CATS Insights. The school is part of the CATS Global Schools network.

CATS Insights is administered by a student editorial team under the oversight of the school.

What personal data is published, and why

CATS Insights publishes only the following personal data about its contributors:

  • The author’s first and last name, in the form they have asked for it to appear
  • The title of the article they have written
  • Their role on the publication, where they hold one

This is published because attribution is a necessary part of academic publication: a reader must be able to identify who wrote a piece of work in order to evaluate and cite it. Authors are credited so that they receive recognition for their work.

CATS Insights does not publish photographs of students, ages, dates of birth, email addresses, telephone numbers, personal social media accounts, or school identification numbers.

Consent

Every contributor, and where the contributor is under 18 their parent or guardian, is asked to give written permission before their name and work are published. Contributors may decline without any consequence for their standing at the school.

Who the information is shared with

  • The public. The edition is published openly on this website and can be read and downloaded by anyone.
  • The CATS Global Schools network. The edition is distributed through CGS channels, including its website, newsletters and social media accounts.
  • Permanent archives. The edition will be deposited with Zenodo, the Internet Archive and the British Library for long-term preservation.

How long it remains available

The edition is intended to remain permanently available. Before the edition is deposited with a permanent archive, an article can be withdrawn from this website on request and the withdrawal takes effect the same day.

After archival deposit, complete removal cannot be guaranteed. Zenodo, the Internet Archive and the British Library each hold their own copies under their own retention policies. Neither CATS Insights nor The Worthgate School controls those copies, and they may not be removable even if a withdrawal request is made.

Contributors who are uncertain about permanent publication should raise it before deposit rather than after.

Withdrawing an article

To withdraw an article, or to correct how a name appears, contact cats-insights@worthgateschool.com. Requests made before archival deposit are actioned on the day they are received.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

This website sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising and no visitor tracking. There are no contact forms and no mailing list. Nothing you do on this site is recorded or profiled.

The site is hosted on Netlify, which processes standard server request information in order to deliver web pages. Headings and body text are rendered using fonts served by Google Fonts.

Your rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to be informed about how your personal data is used, to request access to it, to have inaccurate data corrected, and in certain circumstances to request erasure or to object to processing.

To exercise any of these rights, or to raise a concern about how CATS Insights has handled personal data, contact cats-insights@worthgateschool.com, or contact The Worthgate School directly and ask for the Data Protection Officer.

If you are not satisfied with the response, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
