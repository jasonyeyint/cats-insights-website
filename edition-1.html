How to cite this edition

Cite the edition as a whole:

CATS Insights. (2026). CATS Insights, Edition 1 — September 2026. Canterbury: The Worthgate School. ISSN applied for.

To cite an individual article, give the author and article title, followed by the edition details above and the page range.

Preservation

This edition will be deposited with Zenodo, operated by CERN, which will mint a permanent DOI (Digital Object Identifier) for the edition. A DOI is a stable identifier that continues to resolve to the work even if the website address changes, so the edition remains citable indefinitely. No DOI has been minted yet; it will be added to this page once deposit is complete.

The edition will also be deposited with the Internet Archive and with the British Library, which maintains the UK national collection under legal deposit. An ISSN has been applied for through the ISSN UK Centre at the British Library; until it is assigned, the edition is described as “ISSN applied for”.

Once deposited, copies held by those archives are permanent and may not be removable, including where a withdrawal request is made after deposit.

Contributors who wish to withdraw an article should contact cats-insights@worthgateschool.com before deposit. See the privacy notice for details.

How articles are reviewed

All articles in CATS Insights are reviewed by the student editorial board, which assesses argument, evidence, structure and accuracy, and verifies every citation against its source. Four of the sixteen articles in Edition 1 additionally received subject review by a member of the Worthgate School teaching staff in the relevant discipline.

This is internal editorial review with faculty oversight. It is not external peer review in the COPE sense, and CATS Insights does not claim that for Edition 1.
