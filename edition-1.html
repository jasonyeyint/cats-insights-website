How to cite this edition

Cite the edition as a whole:

To cite an individual article, give the author and article title, followed by the edition details above and the page range.

Preservation

This edition will be deposited with Zenodo, the Internet Archive and the British Library for long-term preservation. Once deposited, copies held by those archives are permanent and may not be removable, including where a withdrawal request is made after deposit.

Contributors who wish to withdraw an article should contact cats-insights@worthgateschool.com before deposit. See the privacy notice for details.

How articles are reviewed

All articles in CATS Insights are reviewed by the student editorial board, which assesses argument, evidence, structure and accuracy, and verifies every citation against its source. Four of the fifteen articles in Edition 1 additionally received subject review by a member of the Worthgate School teaching staff in the relevant discipline.

This is internal editorial review with faculty oversight. It is not external peer review in the COPE sense, and CATS Insights does not claim that for Edition 1.
