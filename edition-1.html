How to cite this edition

CATS Insights, Edition 1 (September 2026). Canterbury: The Worthgate School. ISSN 2979-3890 (Online).

To cite an individual article, give the author and article title, followed by the edition details above and the page range.

Editorial process

Every article in this edition was reviewed and edited by the CATS Insights student editorial board and checked for accuracy, originality and integrity before publication. This is internal editorial review, not external peer review.

Articles were also reviewed by members of staff at The Worthgate School for academic content.

References follow the citation style conventional to each article’s discipline.

Use of artificial intelligence

Every contributor to this edition completed an AI disclosure declaration, held on file by the editorial board. No article in this edition was drafted or written by a large language model (LLM). Disclosed use was limited to grammar checking, fact checking, research assistance and image generation (generated images in figures acknowledged explicitly).
