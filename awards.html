Awards & Recognition

External assessment of the publication’s work, and the evidence submitted in support of it.

RSC Kent Young Chemist Award

CATS Insights submitted a portfolio to the Royal Society of Chemistry Kent Section Young Chemist Award in March 2026. The submission documented the publication’s chemistry writing, its teaching and outreach work, and the mentoring programme through which student writers were trained.

The evidence submitted remains available below.

  • Impact report — outcomes and evaluation
  • Workshop programme — materials and delivery
  • Mentoring programme — structure and assessment
  • Teaching resource — classroom materials
  • Digital outreach — public science communication

John Locke Institute Global Essay Prize

An essay by Kyal Sin Ye Yint, The Fifty-Seven Pence Problem: Non-Substitutability, Irreversibility, and the Case for a Tripartite Environmental Discount Rate, was shortlisted for the 2026 John Locke Institute Global Essay Prize.

The essay appears as the Featured Essay in Edition 1.

Chemistry of Kent

The Chemistry of Kent series examines the chemistry underlying the county’s industry and landscape — hop cultivation and brewing, the carbonate geology of the Dover cliffs, and pharmaceutical research at Sandwich. Three articles from the series appear in Edition 1, and all are readable in full on the blog.
