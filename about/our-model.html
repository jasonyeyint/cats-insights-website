Our Model & How We Work

Structure, process, and the systems that make it function.

Organisational Architecture

The organisation operates on a three-tier hierarchy to prevent decision paralysis, ensure accountability, and give every member clarity on where responsibility lies.

Tier 1 — Strategic

  • President: Kyal Sin Ye Yint (Jason)
  • Vice President: Alex

Responsibility: Direction, editorial vision, faculty relationships, final approval authority. Prevents decision paralysis.

Tier 2 — Operational

The Captains. Managing departments, mentorship, quality control, and week-to-week momentum. Implementation of the core strategy.

Tier 3 — Execution

~22+ General members conducting the intellectual work: Writing, Editing, Designing, and Outreach activities.

The Publication Workflow

Stage 1 — Planning (2 weeks)

Topic selection, recruitment, and theme establishment. Led by President and Captains.

Stage 2 — Writing & Learning (6–8 weeks)

Drafting with mentorship, workshops, and iterative feedback loops. Solo: 500-1000 words. Adv: 2000+.

Stage 3 — Editorial Processing (3–4 weeks)

Editorial review, revisions, Turnitin <15% similarity check, and strict manual authorship verification via Google Docs version history and editorial judgement.

Stage 4 — Design & Layout (2–3 weeks)

Designers carefully format the articles to be released in the official publication.

Stage 5 — Final Review (1–2 weeks)

Final sign-off by the Lead Editor. Selected articles additionally receive subject review by a member of the Worthgate School teaching staff in the relevant discipline.

Stage 6 — Publication & Launch (1 week)

Digital release, social media distribution, and email broadcast across all 7 CATS Global Schools.

Full cycle: approximately 5–6 months per edition.

Weekly Operating Structure

One fixed weekly meeting, with everything else scheduled around contributors’ timetables.

Meeting Logistics

Every Tuesday | 4:05 PM – 5:00 PM | School Library

CATS Insights operates flexibly. The weekly meeting is the fixed point; training courses, lectures and writer induction sessions are scheduled around contributors’ timetables rather than to a fixed calendar, so that new members can join and be trained at any point in the year.

How meetings are run

CATS Insights does not run to a fixed agenda. The agenda for each meeting is set and circulated the day before, based on what the publication needs that week — articles in review, deadlines approaching, training to deliver, or decisions waiting on the team.

The intention is that meeting time is spent on live work rather than on standing items that do not change. Contributors who cannot attend are caught up individually, since training and editorial work are scheduled flexibly in any case.
