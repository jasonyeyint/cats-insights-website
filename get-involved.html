Get Involved

CATS Insights is open to student writers, editors and designers, and to independent contributors outside the school. Submissions for Edition 2 are open now.

Submit an article or essay

CATS Insights publishes work in the sciences, mathematics, engineering and the humanities. Articles are typically between 1,500 and 3,500 words and are pitched at an educated non-specialist reader: rigorous, but readable by someone who is not a specialist in the field.

To propose a piece, email cats-insights@worthgateschool.com with:

  • A working title and a paragraph describing the argument
  • The discipline the piece sits in
  • Two or three sources you expect to build on
  • Your name, and your school or institution

A full draft is not needed to pitch. A clear idea is enough. Proposals are read by the editorial board and you will receive a reply either way.

What happens after you submit

  • 1. ProposalThe editorial board reviews your idea and confirms the scope and word count with you.
  • 2. DraftingYou write, with a named editor available for questions. Writers new to academic writing are paired with a mentor.
  • 3. Structural editAn editor reviews argument, evidence and structure, and identifies gaps.
  • 4. Line editA second pass on precision, clarity and voice.
  • 5. VerificationEvery citation is checked against the original source. Claims that cannot be supported are removed.
  • 6. PublicationThe piece is designed, proofed and published in the next edition under your name.

Join the team

Contributors join as writers, editors or designers. No prior experience is required for any of these roles — training is provided, and several current editors joined with no publishing background.

  • Writers research and draft articles in a discipline of their choosing.
  • Editors assess argument and evidence, verify citations and work with writers through revisions.
  • Designers produce diagrams, figures and layout for the published edition.

Training runs as short courses and lectures scheduled around contributors’ timetables rather than to a fixed calendar, so it is possible to join and be trained at any point in the year.

Contact

For submissions, membership, or to discuss working with CATS Insights, email cats-insights@worthgateschool.com.

The editorial team meets every Tuesday, 4:05–5:00 PM, in the School Library. Prospective contributors are welcome to come along.

A note for contributors under 18

Before a piece is published, written permission is required from the contributor and, where the contributor is under 18, from a parent or guardian. Only a contributor’s name and article title are published — never ages, photographs or contact details. The privacy notice sets out exactly what is published and how to withdraw a piece.
